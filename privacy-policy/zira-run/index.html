Privacy Policy - Zira Run

Last updated: October 29th, 2026

Zira Run is developed and published under the Pinsilabs brand by Sulis Triyono, an independent developer based in Indonesia.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Zira Run. In this policy, "Pinsilabs," "we," "our," and "us" refer to the individual operating under the Pinsilabs brand.

1. Information We Collect

Zira Run does not currently collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal information.

The game does not require you to create an account or provide personal details such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Zira Run stores certain game information locally on your device, including:

  • Your highest score
  • Your remaining health
  • Health recovery time
  • Sound effects preference
  • Vibration preference
  • Notification preference
  • Other settings required for the game to function

This information is stored only on your device using local application storage. Pinsilabs does not receive or have access to this information.

Your locally stored data may be removed if you clear the app's storage, uninstall the game, or reset your device.

3. Notifications

With your permission, Zira Run may send a local notification when your in-game health has fully recovered.

Health recovery times and notification schedules are processed locally on your device. This information is not sent to Pinsilabs.

On supported devices, Zira Run will request permission before sending notifications. You can enable or disable notifications from:

  • The Options screen inside Zira Run
  • Your device's application notification settings

Disabling notifications does not prevent you from playing the game.

4. Vibration

Zira Run may use your device's vibration feature to provide feedback during gameplay, such as when the player collides with an obstacle.

You can enable or disable vibration from the Options screen. The vibration feature does not collect or transmit personal information.

5. Sound Effects

Zira Run includes sound effects for gameplay and menu interactions. You can enable or disable sound effects from the Options screen.

This setting is stored locally on your device.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Zira Run does not currently include:

  • Third-party advertising
  • Behavioural tracking
  • Analytics services
  • Marketing trackers
  • Crash-reporting services that send personal information to Pinsilabs

If any of these services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

7. Purchases, Donations and Tips

Zira Run does not currently process purchases, donations, or tips.

The game may display interface elements related to supporting Pinsilabs, but no payment is processed unless a payment feature is explicitly made available.

If purchases or optional tips are introduced in the future, payments may be processed by the relevant application store or payment provider. Pinsilabs will update this Privacy Policy before such a feature becomes available.

8. Data Sharing

Because Zira Run does not currently collect personal information, Pinsilabs does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, legal proceedings, or a valid request from a government authority. However, Pinsilabs cannot disclose locally stored game information that it does not receive or control.

9. Data Security

Game progress and preferences are stored locally using the storage facilities provided by your device's operating system.

Although reasonable care is taken when developing Zira Run, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information remains on your device until it is removed.

You can delete this information by:

  1. Opening your device settings.
  2. Finding Zira Run in the application list.
  3. Selecting the option to clear the app's storage or data.

You can also remove locally stored information by uninstalling Zira Run.

Because Pinsilabs does not receive this locally stored information, you do not need to contact us to request its deletion.

11. Children's Privacy

Zira Run does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The game does not require an account and does not ask users to submit personal information. If a future version introduces features that collect personal information, this policy and the game's age-related protections will be reviewed and updated accordingly.

Parents or guardians with questions about Zira Run may contact us using the details below.

12. Third-Party Platforms

Zira Run may be downloaded through third-party platforms such as Google Play. Those platforms may independently collect information when you download, install, purchase, or update an application.

Information collected directly by an application store is governed by that platform's own privacy policy and is not controlled by Pinsilabs.

13. International Users

Zira Run may be available to users in different countries. The game's locally stored information remains on the user's device and is not transferred to Pinsilabs.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Zira Run's features, legal requirements, or information-handling practices change.

Updates will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
